## 📌 Project Overview

**EcomIQ – Smart E-Commerce Intelligence** is an end-to-end **Power BI business intelligence project** designed to analyze e-commerce sales, customer behaviour, product performance, orders, payments, and customer retention.

The project transforms raw e-commerce data into an interactive dashboard that helps identify business trends, high-performing products and customers, operational issues, and opportunities for growth.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focuses on six major analytical areas:

1. **Sales Performance Analysis**
   - Analyze overall revenue and sales trends
   - Identify high-performing cities and categories
   - Track monthly sales performance

2. **Customer Behaviour**
   - Understand customer purchasing patterns
   - Identify active and repeat customers
   - Analyze customer value

3. **Product & Inventory Performance**
   - Identify top-performing products
   - Analyze category performance
   - Monitor inventory levels

4. **Order & Payment Analysis**
   - Analyze order status and delivery performance
   - Identify cancellation patterns
   - Understand payment method usage

5. **Customer Value & Retention**
   - Measure repeat customer behaviour
   - Identify valuable customer segments
   - Understand customer retention patterns

6. **Executive Summary**
   - Provide a high-level overview of important KPIs
   - Present actionable business insights and recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights

### Sales Performance

- The overall revenue generated is approximately **₹116.15M**.
- **Electronics** is the leading product category.
- **Sports** is another strong-performing category.
- **Mumbai and Bengaluru** are among the major revenue-generating cities.
- **April and August** show strong revenue performance.

### Customer Behaviour

- The project has **100 customers**, with **99 active customers**.
- **95 customers are repeat customers**.
- The repeat customer rate of **95%** indicates strong customer engagement and purchasing behaviour.

### Product & Inventory

- Electronics products contribute significantly to overall revenue.
- High-performing products include:
  - Eco Similique
  - Eco A
  - Smart Iste
- Inventory analysis shows:
  - **70% healthy stock**
  - **24% medium stock**
  - **6% low stock**

### Orders & Payments

- **349 orders** were delivered.
- Delivery rate is approximately **69.80%**.
- **53 orders** were cancelled.
- Cancellation rate is approximately **10.60%**.
- Net Banking and Debit Card are among the major payment methods.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen High-Performing Categories

Focus marketing campaigns and inventory planning around strong categories such as **Electronics and Sports**.

### 2. Improve Customer Loyalty

With a high repeat customer rate, the business can introduce:

- Loyalty rewards
- Personalized offers
- Repeat-purchase incentives
- VIP customer programs

### 3. Reduce Order Cancellations

Investigate the causes behind cancelled orders, particularly in major revenue-generating locations, and identify opportunities to improve order confirmation and fulfilment.

### 4. Improve Delivery Performance

A delivery rate of **69.80%** suggests an opportunity to improve logistics, fulfilment, and delivery operations.

### 5. Monitor Low Inventory

The **6% low-stock inventory** should be monitored through stock alerts and proactive replenishment to reduce the risk of stockouts.

### 6. Use Sales Trends for Planning

Monthly sales trends can be used to improve:

- Promotional campaigns
- Inventory planning
- Seasonal offers
- Marketing allocation

### 7. Focus on High-Value Customers

Customer-level analysis can help identify valuable customers and support targeted retention strategies.
